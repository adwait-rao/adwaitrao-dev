# Why Go is great for tooling

Go gives you a **single compiled binary** with absolutely zero external runtime requirements. 

## Key Learnings Today:
* **Strict typing:** Structs ensure your markdown data maps perfectly to code.
* **Separation of concerns:** Notes in `/content/notes` aren't touched by the compilation loop, keeping personal thoughts safe.
